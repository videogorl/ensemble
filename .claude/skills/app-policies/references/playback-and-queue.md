# Playback And Queue Policy

Load this reference for playback start behavior, queue state, shuffle/repeat/autoplay, offline queue filtering, local-file playback, transport recovery, prefetch, reporting, or system-media donation behavior.

## Policies

- `PlaybackService` holds the single live source of truth for queue order, current index, sections, history, shuffle, repeat, and autoplay state. Queue collaborators operate on values supplied by the service and must not publish or retain a second live queue.
- On iOS/iPadOS 18+, `PlaybackService` preserves Apple Music and Plex tracks in one provider-neutral logical queue. Play, Shuffle, Radio, restoration, bulk queue mutations, Play Next, and Play Last keep their mixed-source order. Finite playback submits one logical Apple Music item at a time to the app-scoped `ApplicationMusicPlayer` through `AppleMusicPlaybackController`; Apple Music stations may retain their native dynamic queue, and all other tracks play through Ensemble's audio engine. Automatic advancement, explicit navigation, and restoration may cross provider boundaries without replacing or filtering the logical queue.
- Apple Music/Plex boundaries are hard, non-overlapping handoffs built only from public MusicKit, AVAudioSession, and UIKit background-task APIs. Stop Ensemble's audio engine and configure its playback session as mixable before starting or resuming `ApplicationMusicPlayer`. At Apple-to-native boundaries, stop and clear `ApplicationMusicPlayer`, activate Ensemble's session while it remains mixable so the claim is legal in the background, then restore nonmixable long-form `.playback` before starting the native engine so Ensemble regains normal system-control ownership without reconfiguring a live stream. The transition background lease belongs to the latest playback generation; acquire it during the final four seconds of an Apple Music segment boundary or an unscheduled native boundary so a locked device cannot suspend between providers. Never intentionally overlap, gaplessly join, crossfade, decode, export, analyze, or mix Apple Music PCM with other-source audio.
- `ApplicationMusicPlayer` renders Apple Music audio while Ensemble publishes provider-neutral metadata and command availability through `MPNowPlayingInfoCenter` and `MPRemoteCommandCenter`. Each finite Apple item uses a disposable one-item MusicKit queue, so remote Next ends that native queue and `AppleMusicPlaybackController` advances exactly once to the next logical item regardless of provider. MusicKit consumes Previous itself; after the standard restart-at-zero gesture, `PlaybackService` recognizes a second near-start rewind and applies the same provider-neutral Previous policy, including Apple-to-Plex boundaries. iOS/iPadOS 15–17 remain Plex-only because Ensemble's Apple Music playback provider requires iOS 18.
- Resume rebuilds the current Apple Music segment when its native queue is unavailable, and catalog tracks remain playable whether or not they are in the user's library or associated with a reachable Plex server. Repeated catalog IDs end the current MusicKit segment so duplicate queue entries remain independently addressable. Future-order mutations discard the submitted MusicKit queue after its current entry without interrupting current playback; the next logical segment is rebuilt at the natural boundary. Successful station-entry removal edits the native station queue directly. Catalog-backed library tracks retry through their library identifier when a stale catalog relationship no longer resolves. The selected first item must resolve; later items confirmed absent are pruned from only that submitted segment without failing its playable items. A transient lookup error ends the submitted segment before the affected item so its logical queue entry remains in order and is retried at the boundary. Queue replacement stops and clears the prior MusicKit queue before resolving the new segment and ignores its transient stopped/current-entry notifications while the replacement prepares, so a failed replacement cannot leave stale native audio playing. `AppleMusicPlaybackController` cancels and generation-gates asynchronous play, resume, and station-start work: Stop, Pause, and every newer command invalidate older completions; Pause during preparation leaves no resumable native queue; an older error must never stop a newer MusicKit queue; and an older MusicKit call returning after Stop must reassert the stopped intent. Because finite and station playback share one application player, a stale completion must never stop a newer queue. Every MusicKit callback and Ensemble-engine first-render callback carries its playback generation; stale stream loads, completions, failures, seeks, and prefetch work must not mutate or stop a newer request. A task-based main-actor monitor observes finite playback while the app is backgrounded, polls resolved and transient queue entries to self-heal missed metadata notifications, advances once the last queued song reaches its duration, and de-duplicates that fallback against native paused/stopped end signals. Paused or stopped end signals are confirmed after a short delay and suppressed during interruptions until MusicKit is observed playing again.
- MusicKit owns Apple Music audio rendering, AirPlay routing, and audio-session interruptions while it is active; `PlaybackService` owns sequencing between finite logical items. Ensemble's local two-deck SmartMix and gapless scheduling remain Plex-only. When Apple Music is enabled, both SmartMix settings layouts state that SmartMix cannot transition between Apple Music and other services. Apple Music tracks publish an empty waveform/frequency snapshot and do not expose Lyrics or instrumental mode. `WaveformView` renders that empty state as one joined progress rail and cross-fades it into waveform bars if samples later become available.
- Regular play disables shuffle and starts from the requested index. Shuffle play enables shuffle, preserves original order for restore, and starts from the shuffled queue.
- Toggling shuffle keeps the current item, excludes autoplay items from the shuffle candidates, filters already played history from candidates, and restores original order when disabled.
- Queue navigation records history before advancing, including the final item when the queue exhausts. Provider callbacks for a repeated track identity prefer the matching occurrence after the current index so replaying a History item cannot jump backward into a cycle. Previous restarts the current track when invoked after the configured restart threshold and wraps only when repeat-all is enabled.
- Autoplay is a separate queue section. It should not be treated as manually queued content or shuffled into the main future queue.
- Apple Music autoplay prepares the seed song's MusicKit station, advances past the already-finished seed before starting playback, and replaces any existing Autoplay suffix when the final manual queue item is Apple Music. Exposed MusicKit station entries populate Ensemble's Autoplay section, refresh after native skips, and removals from that section must also remove the corresponding future MusicKit queue entry. If a lightweight or restored Apple Music entry omits artwork, the current entry is enriched from the catalog so Now Playing controls receive the artwork without changing queue identity. MusicKit-only transient artwork URLs must be replaced with resolvable catalog album artwork before reaching shared artwork loaders. Ensemble-engine autoplay never falls back to Apple Music.
- The first `Play Next` action inserts immediately after the current item. Later `Play Next` actions append to the existing `upNext` section in action order, before `continuePlaying` and `autoplay` items; multi-track actions preserve their internal order.
- Moving a future queue item between `Up Next` and `Continue Playing` adopts the destination section and updates the table's section model synchronously with the native reorder.
- A queue is protected after a manual `Play Next`, `Play Last`, add-to-queue, removal, or reorder. A direct app-UI Play, Shuffle, or Radio start must confirm before replacing that protected queue; Cancel leaves it untouched. Siri, App Shortcuts, remote commands, autoplay, restoration, and background recovery do not present confirmation UI.
- Autoplay lookahead limits and cleanup apply only to `.autoplay` queue items. Enabling or refreshing Autoplay must never trim `continuePlaying` or `upNext` items, regardless of manual queue length.
- SmartMix is a per-device playback preference controlled from the Now Playing Queue card and Profile's SmartMix settings. It defaults off, persists in local `UserDefaults`, and does not sync across devices. The settings page defaults to disabling SmartMix between consecutive tracks from the same source-scoped album; this keeps those boundaries gapless in every queue context.
- SmartMix uses bounded local/temp file silence and tempo analysis, equal-power overlapping deck fades, an eased outgoing high-pass sweep, and confidence-gated tempo matching. Very high-confidence matches let the outgoing deck ease toward the incoming track's tempo during the overlap; moderate-confidence close matches may still use incoming-only time stretching. SmartMix automatically falls back to non-stretched overlap when confidence, rate range, or device state is not suitable.
- SmartMix uses a true two-deck playback model: when a transition finishes, the incoming deck remains the live deck until the next transition, and the next SmartMix schedules onto the opposite deck. Do not force an immediate handoff back to a single primary deck at the transition boundary.
- SmartMix transitions keep track A current until transition midpoint, then promote track B for Now Playing presentation, artwork, queue index, system media identifiers, timeline reporting, and history. At that midpoint, Now Playing dissolves the outgoing album art into the incoming art; ordinary track changes remain immediate. A skip before the SmartMix threshold keeps B playing; a skip at or after the threshold advances past B.
- Now Playing shows a centered `Mixing` status during an active SmartMix overlap. Scrubbing takes precedence and temporarily replaces that status with the existing scrub-rate indicator.
- SmartMix must gracefully fall back to existing gapless playback when analysis, file resolution, queue shape, route recovery, or second-deck scheduling is unavailable.
- SmartMix remains available on constrained devices. Analysis results are cached by track identity plus file metadata and analysis work routes through foreground budgeting so tempo/silence analysis does not run concurrently with startup sync, share sheets, Now Playing gestures, or audio-critical sections on A9/iOS 15-class devices.
- A prefetched SmartMix track waits until its transition window without rebuilding the same plan on every playback-clock tick. Queue changes and seeks past the transition window must still make scheduling eligible immediately.
- Playback route recovery must preserve the user-visible playhead. Audio-engine scheduling anchors, such as deck segment offsets used for SmartMix or gapless playback, must not become resume positions when render timing is unavailable.
- System Now Playing artwork should not flash to generated fallback artwork during track transitions. When the incoming track has an artwork path, keep the previous system artwork until the new artwork loads, then replace it; only use generated fallback artwork when no artwork path exists or the artwork load fails. Reuse resolved artwork when consecutive tracks share the same source-scoped primary-and-fallback resolution identity.
- Playback start paths must pass `PlaybackStartContext`. Only direct app UI starts donate to system media; Siri, App Shortcuts, remote commands, autoplay, restoration, and background recovery are non-donating.
- macOS Dock menu playback controls are user commands for the existing queue/playback state. They must dispatch through `PlaybackService`/active Now Playing owners and must not add system-media donations or mutate `MPRemoteCommandCenter` directly.
- Plex playback chooses the highest-quality playable payload under the current connectivity policy. Offline, limited-connectivity, or cellular-with-streaming-disabled playback is local-only. Low Data Mode prefers a valid local file even when the configured stream quality is higher. Otherwise, stream only when the requested streaming quality strictly outranks the actual downloaded-file quality; equal or lower streaming quality stays local, and a failed higher-quality stream falls back to the valid local file. Corrupt or invalid local files fail locally when streaming is unavailable and may recover by streaming or refreshing when it is allowed.
- The Now Playing `InfoCard` File section describes the payload actually loaded by `AudioPlaybackEngine`, not the current track's download availability. `PlaybackService.currentPlaybackFileInfo()` supplies playing codec, complete payload size, resolved source, requested quality, and decoded sample rate; Plex metadata remains separately and explicitly labeled as Original. Omit unresolved engine facts rather than inferring them from library metadata.
- Network playback should start from incremental `PlaybackSource` streams instead of waiting for complete temp files. Direct Plex file streams and PMS universal transcodes decode through `AudioPlaybackEngine` via the streaming decoder/pipeline; write-through cache files are follow-on assets for analysis, replay, and local scheduling, not startup prerequisites.
- Active incremental streams must not fake seek success. If the active decoder cannot seek in place, seeking must restart the current queue item through a source-level repositioning path such as PMS transcode `offset`; completed cache files may still use file-backed seeks.
- Active incremental stream failures, including sustained PCM starvation before or after the first audible render, must stop silent rendering and restart the current queue item from its visible playhead; intentional stream cancellation during queue changes must not trigger recovery.
- Now Playing waveform state should distinguish played, buffered/loaded, and not-yet-loaded regions. Streaming decks report decoded buffer progress through `PlaybackService.bufferedProgress`; streams that start at a nonzero source offset must rebase decoded progress onto the absolute track timeline before publishing. `WaveformView` renders not-yet-loaded regions with lower opacity.
- Device-offline queues are filtered to downloaded Plex tracks; Apple Music entries remain eligible so MusicKit can use Apple's available local or network asset. Device-online queues skip non-downloaded Plex tracks from known-unavailable servers; unknown or in-flight health states must not clear or strand an existing queue.
- Direct file streams and universal transcode can both be valid. Do not disable either broadly without live endpoint proof and a scoped failing path.
- Watch-local playback requests original-quality direct file streams because its compact player uses `AVPlayer`. Never pass a progressive universal-transcode `start.mp3` URL to Watch `AVPlayer`; that chunked response requires the main app's incremental playback pipeline.
- Watch Now Playing must observe the local playback controller directly so its transport state and clock stay synchronized with the active player.
- Phone-owned Apple Music playback is remote-control-only on Watch. Current metadata and transport state may cross WatchConnectivity; Apple Music source configuration, library cache, catalog search, and playlist data must not.
- Queue restore and live source changes use the same source snapshot. Enforce a known provider's enabled sources as soon as that provider is authoritative, while preserving valid unresolved-provider items only until that provider's configuration settles. Missing or malformed source keys are invalid and must be pruned from queue, original queue, and history without owner inference; also prune disabled sources once authoritative, advance to the next proven-enabled item when necessary, and persist the repaired snapshot.
- Watch-local playback keeps one in-memory indexed queue for the selected detail, uses `AVQueuePlayer` to preload one direct-file item for gapless transitions, and cancels stale stream requests when the user changes tracks or queues. If preloading is unavailable, playback resolves the next track after the current item ends. Regular play starts at the requested track; Shuffle randomizes the full queue before playback. Previous restarts after three seconds or moves to the prior queue entry near the start, including duplicate entries; Next does not wrap past the final track.
- Watch-local playback must configure `AVAudioSession` for `.playback` with `.longFormAudio` and use the asynchronous route-aware activation API before starting `AVPlayer`, so eligible routes continue through wrist-down/sleep and receive the system's active-background-session glyph. It publishes source-scoped track, artwork, and queue metadata through `MPNowPlayingInfoCenter`, registers only the supported play/pause/next/previous commands with `MPRemoteCommandCenter`, and pauses for interruptions or loss of the active audio route. Register only the active target's remote-command handlers so a paused Watch-local player cannot consume iPhone-remote commands. `WatchNowPlayingView` routes its native-toolbar transport and More actions to the active local or iPhone owner, while `WKInterfaceVolumeControl` owns Digital Crown system volume for that target; never replace Crown volume with `digitalCrownRotation` or a player-volume binding. Because paired simulators don't expose the companion MediaRemote origin, simulator builds may mirror the existing WatchConnectivity snapshot, size-capped compressed current artwork, and commands into the Watch's system Now Playing session so remote metadata and transport remain testable without blocking state delivery; physical builds must continue using the system companion source.
- Watch remote-control replies carry only command acceptance or errors; artwork-backed state stays on the application-context snapshot channel. Transport buttons remain available while replies are pending so rapid commands such as double-Previous work.
- Playback resolution, timeline reporting, and scrobbling must remain provider-exact. Track operations require an exact library-scoped source and never accept or infer ownership from a server-scoped key. A valid server-scoped item such as a playlist may resolve to a registered provider only on the same provider/account/server; missing or malformed source keys fail explicitly, and routing never crosses a provider or server boundary.
- Source-less Siri handoffs may use name/fuzzy matching within enabled sources, but must not use direct media IDs to infer a source owner. A Siri handoff with an explicit source may use name/fuzzy recovery only inside that source scope; a stale, unavailable, malformed, or wrong source never falls through to a same-named item from another source.
- Lyrics loading should try all regular, non-chord lyric streams exposed by current track metadata in priority order, preferring timed streams while keeping plain/local sidecars as fallbacks. Local regular sidecars should use the raw lyrics fetch path so plain text and LRC content are preserved.
- Lyrics chord mode is queue-scoped UI state. Enabling chord mode applies to the current queue until the queue item identity sequence changes; queue rebuilds reset it to off. When enabled, tracks without chord streams fall back to normal lyrics while preserving the queue-scoped enabled state so later chord-capable tracks resume chord display automatically.

## Owners

- `PlaybackService` remains the playback facade, owns the live logical queue, provider segmentation and hard handoffs, and is the side-effect boundary for queue mutation and transport retry loops.
- `PlaybackQueueController` owns focused pure queue/history transformations and coordinates snapshot persistence and download-state restamping using state supplied by `PlaybackService`; it does not own a parallel live queue.
- `PlaybackQueueStore` owns backward-compatible queue/history snapshot serialization. Full queue snapshots use one atomic Application Support file for queue and lifecycle changes; periodic playhead checkpoints use a small sidecar so active playback does not repeatedly rewrite the full queue payload. Existing `UserDefaults` snapshots migrate once after a successful file write and their legacy keys are removed.
- `NowPlayingViewModel` gates app-UI queue replacements, while `RootView` and `DownloadsPresentationContainer` present the shared native replacement confirmation.
- `PlaybackTransportCoordinator`, `PlaybackRecoveryPolicy`, `PlaybackLocalFilePolicy`, `PlaybackPrefetchController`, `PlaybackLaunchCoordinator`, `StreamingAudioPipeline`, `StreamingAudioDecoder`, `StreamingPCMBuffer`, `SmartMixAnalysisService`, and `SmartMixPlanner` own focused playback seams.
- `PlaybackAudioSessionCoordinator` owns queue-appropriate playback-category configuration, route-aware activation, and audio-session interruption and route-change observation.
- `AppleMusicPlaybackController` is the iOS 18 MusicKit transport adapter for the currently submitted Apple Music segment. It never owns a second Ensemble queue or advances across a provider boundary.
- `ForegroundWorkScheduler` owns playback-safe budgeting for optional analysis work; it must not block user-initiated playback commands or download transfers.
- `PlaybackNowPlayingBridge` owns Ensemble's manual `MPNowPlayingInfoCenter` and `MPRemoteCommandCenter` writes, including deferral while MusicKit owns an Apple Music segment.
- `SystemMediaIntegrationService` owns donations, Spotlight indexing/deletion, and media user-context refresh.

## Implementation Hooks

- Keep MediaPlayer singleton writes out of main-app playback engines, view models, and UI. The independent Watch client keeps its small Now Playing bridge in `WatchPlaybackController` rather than importing `EnsembleCore`.
- Do not filter the logical queue by playback engine. Provider-specific native queues are disposable transport segments rebuilt from `PlaybackService` state.
- Keep playback queue snapshots backward compatible when persistence changes.
- Use source-scoped identifiers for Now Playing external IDs, donations, Spotlight identifiers, artwork cache identity, and reporting.
- When changing streaming or playback transport, load `plex-api` and test the relevant PMS endpoint with curl using `.env` credentials.

## Verification

- Add or update queue tests for shuffle, repeat, autoplay, history, queue restoration, and download-state refresh changes.
- Add or update Now Playing tests when queue-scoped UI state such as Lyrics chord mode must persist across track advance and reset on queue rebuild.
- Add playback transport/recovery tests for local-file validation, offline fallback, endpoint selection, and retry classification changes.
- Add mixed-provider queue tests for order, mutations, restoration, provider-segment boundaries, stale callback rejection, and exactly-once advancement. Validate Apple Music-to-Plex and Plex-to-Apple handoffs on a physical iPhone while foregrounded, backgrounded, and locked; verify the exact current build and capture audible output plus timestamped transport/audio-session logs.
- Use simulator validation for user-visible playback, Now Playing, remote command, or unavailable-track behavior.
